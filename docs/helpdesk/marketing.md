## Fiverr gig description
**I will build an AI-powered helpdesk or support portal for your business**

Drowning in support email? I'll build you a support desk where AI does the sorting and the first draft, and your
team stays in control.

What you get:
- A branded support portal where your customers open and track requests
- A team inbox sorted by urgency, with assignments, internal notes and an activity log
- AI that labels every request (topic, priority, mood) the moment it arrives
- One-click reply drafts written only from your own help articles, with sources shown
- Conversation summaries for fast handovers
- Usage limits so AI costs never surprise you; works with free open-source models or the provider you choose
- Secure sign-in, roles and permissions, and data separated per company

Packages (scope, not price):
- **Basic** — support portal + team inbox + knowledge base, email notifications, deployed to your hosting.
- **Standard** — Basic + AI triage, AI reply drafts from your articles, summaries, usage dashboard.
- **Premium** — Standard + your channels (support email, website chat), custom routing rules and SLAs, Stripe
  plans for your own customers, an accuracy check of the AI on your past tickets, and handover documentation.
