## Upwork portfolio blurb (≤150 words)
**Real-time booking system (Next.js + Postgres) with AI booking assistant**

A production-style scheduling app for appointment businesses. Live availability over Server-Sent Events, 5-minute
slot holds, and a Postgres exclusion constraint that makes double booking impossible: when 4 customers race for one
slot in a test, exactly 1 wins and the rest get the nearest alternatives. Time-zone and DST-correct slot engine,
reschedule/cancel with optimistic concurrency, a live staff calendar, activity feed and notifications. The AI
assistant turns "deep tissue with Sam next Tuesday after 4pm" into real open slots: the model extracts intent, code
resolves dates and times, and nothing invented reaches the app (89.5% of 19 test requests fully correct). The
owner's weekly digest flags any AI-written number that isn't in the data. 91 integration + 18 browser tests;
Lighthouse mobile 90/100/100/100. Demo: [DEMO LINK]

## Fiverr gig description
**I will build a custom online booking system with live availability for your business**

Stop double bookings and phone tag. Let customers book in seconds, and see your whole day at a glance.

What you get:
- Online booking for your services and team, on your own website (not a rented template)
- Live availability: times disappear the moment someone else books
- Guaranteed no double bookings, including clean-up time between appointments
- Customers can reschedule or cancel themselves, up to the cut-off you choose
- A live team calendar with no-show tracking, activity history and notifications
- Correct handling of time zones and daylight-saving changes

Packages (scope, not price):
- **Basic** — services, staff, opening hours, booking with live availability, customer accounts, my-bookings page.
- **Standard** — Basic + team calendar, reschedule/cancel rules, notifications, activity feed, admin for hours and time off.
- **Premium** — Standard + AI booking assistant, weekly owner digest, deposits via Stripe, email/SMS reminders and calendar sync.
