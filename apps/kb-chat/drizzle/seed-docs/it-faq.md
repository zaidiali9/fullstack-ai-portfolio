# IT Help FAQ

SEED DATA: written for the Cairn demo (fictional company Brightline Studio).

## How do I connect to the VPN?

Install the TunnelGate client from the IT portal and sign in with your work account. Multi-factor authentication is required. The VPN is mandatory when working with client files outside the office.

## What is the guest Wi-Fi password?

The guest network is called Brightline-Guest. Its password rotates every Monday and is posted at reception. Employees should use the Brightline-Staff network instead.

## How do I request new software?

Request software through the IT portal under "Software request". Requests are usually approved within 2 business days. Software that processes client data also needs a security review.

## When is IT support available?

IT support is available Monday to Friday from 8:00 to 18:00. Call extension 4400 or open a ticket in the IT portal. For security emergencies outside these hours, follow the Security Incident Response guide.

## How do I print?

Printers are on every floor. Send jobs to "Brightline-Print" and release them with your badge at any printer.
