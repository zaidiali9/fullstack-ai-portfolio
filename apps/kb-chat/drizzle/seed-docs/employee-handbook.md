# Brightline Studio Employee Handbook

SEED DATA: Brightline Studio is a fictional design agency created for this demo. This handbook was written for the Cairn portfolio project.

## Working hours

Brightline uses flexible hours. Everyone is expected to be available during core hours, 10:00 to 15:00 in their local time zone, Monday to Friday. Outside core hours you can arrange your day as you like, as long as you work about 40 hours per week and keep your calendar up to date.

## Remote work

You can work remotely up to 3 days per week. Fully remote arrangements are possible but require written approval from your manager and the head of people. When working remotely, use the company VPN for any client files.

## Vacation

Full-time employees receive 25 vacation days per year, accrued monthly (about 2.08 days per month). Request time off in the People portal at least two weeks in advance for absences longer than three days. Up to 5 unused vacation days can be carried over into the next year; carried-over days must be used by March 31, after which they expire.

## Sick leave

You have 10 paid sick days per year. Tell your manager as early as possible on the first day of absence. A doctor's note is required after 3 consecutive sick days.

## Parental leave

The primary caregiver receives 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave. The secondary caregiver receives 6 weeks of fully paid leave, which can be taken any time during the first year after birth or adoption.

## Equipment

Every employee gets a laptop, which is refreshed every 3 years. New hires also receive a one-time home office stipend of $500 for a desk, chair or monitor. Lost or stolen equipment must be reported to IT immediately (see the Security Incident Response guide).

## Learning and development

Each employee has a learning budget of $1,200 per calendar year for courses, books and conferences. Unused budget does not roll over. Ask your manager to approve conference trips that also require travel.

## Performance reviews

Performance reviews take place twice a year, in June and December. Reviews include a self-assessment, feedback from two peers and a conversation with your manager about goals for the next six months.
