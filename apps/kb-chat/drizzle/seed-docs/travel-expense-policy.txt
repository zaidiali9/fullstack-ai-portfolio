Travel and Expense Policy
SEED DATA: written for the Cairn demo (fictional company Brightline Studio). Rendered to a 3-page PDF by the seed script; pages are separated by a form feed.

Booking travel
All business travel must be booked through the TravelDesk portal at least 14 days in advance whenever possible. Economy class is required for flights shorter than 6 hours. Business class is allowed for flights longer than 6 hours, but only with written approval from a director. Hotels should be booked at the negotiated corporate rate; if no corporate hotel is available, choose a mid-range hotel within walking distance of the client office. Rental cars need manager approval and must be the smallest practical size.

Meals and per diem
Client dinners are reimbursable up to $75 per person, including tax and tip. Write the names of the attendees on the receipt. When you travel without a client meal, you receive a daily per diem of $60 for domestic trips and $80 for international trips instead of submitting meal receipts. Alcohol is not reimbursable, except at client dinners, where it is limited to two drinks per person.

Submitting expenses
Submit expenses within 30 days of the purchase in the Ledger app. Attach an itemized receipt for every expense over $25. Approved expenses are reimbursed in the next payroll cycle. Personal car use for business is reimbursed at $0.67 per mile; log the start and end address of each trip. Expenses submitted after 90 days are not reimbursed.
