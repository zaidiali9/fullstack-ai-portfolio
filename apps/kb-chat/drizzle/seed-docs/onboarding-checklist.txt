New Hire Onboarding Checklist
SEED DATA: written for the Cairn demo (fictional company Brightline Studio).

Before your first day
Sign your contract and tax forms in the People portal. Choose your laptop (Mac or Windows) by replying to the IT welcome email.

Day 1
Collect your laptop and badge from reception at 9:30. Set up Vaultkeeper and multi-factor authentication with IT. Have lunch with your onboarding buddy, who is assigned before you start.

Week 1
Read the Employee Handbook and the Security Incident Response guide. Complete the 45-minute security awareness course. Meet your manager to agree on goals for your first 90 days.

First month
Shadow at least two client calls. Order home office equipment with your $500 stipend. Book a 30-day check-in with your manager.
