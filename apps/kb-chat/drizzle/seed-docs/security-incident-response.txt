Security Incident Response Guide
SEED DATA: written for the Cairn demo (fictional company Brightline Studio). Converted to a Word (.docx) document by the seed script.

Reporting an incident
If you suspect a security incident, report it within 1 hour by emailing security@brightline.example or posting in the #sec-incidents channel. Do not try to investigate on your own and do not power off an affected device, because evidence in memory can be lost; disconnect it from the network instead.

Severity levels
SEV1 means customer data is exposed or a core system is down; the on-call security lead responds within 15 minutes. SEV2 means a contained compromise such as a single infected laptop; response within 4 hours. SEV3 covers suspicious activity with no confirmed impact; response by the next business day.

Phishing
Forward suspicious emails as attachments to phishing@brightline.example and then delete them. Never click links or open attachments in a message you are unsure about.

Lost or stolen devices
Report a lost or stolen laptop or phone immediately, even outside working hours. IT will remotely wipe the device and revoke its access tokens. File a police report for stolen devices and share the reference number with IT.

Passwords and access
Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. The Vaultkeeper password manager is mandatory for all work accounts, and multi-factor authentication is required on email, the VPN and all client systems.
